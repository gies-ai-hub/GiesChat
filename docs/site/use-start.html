Getting started

GiesChat helps you work through course concepts, not just produce an answer. Here's how to sign in and send your first message.

1. Sign in

Go to GiesChat and choose Sign in with Illinois. You use your normal NetID and password through Illinois Microsoft sign-in, so there's no separate GiesChat account to create.

2. Take the tour

The first time you sign in, a short tour walks you through choosing a model, browsing course tutors and reporting a problem. To see it again, open More in the left sidebar and choose Take the tour.

3. Pick a model

The model picker sits in the message box. Each model lists what it's best at under its name, and you can switch at any time.

Choose your model

GPT-6.1 Sol Most capable: tough problem sets, math, long documents, and careful analysis.
Claude Sonnet 5 Quick and sharp: strong at writing, editing, and code.
Claude Opus 4.8 Most thorough: long documents, complex analysis, and the hardest problems.

GPT models can also search the web.

4. Use a course tutor

Open Agents in the sidebar to browse tutors built around the concepts and vocabulary of Gies courses, grouped under Finance, Accounting and Business Administration. A tutor answers from its course's material and explains the reasoning rather than handing over an answer.

Academic integrity: every model in GiesChat is instructed to support learning: short answers, explanations over finished assignments. Your course's own AI policy still applies.

Where your chats go

Every conversation is saved in the left sidebar so you can pick up where you left off. Use New chat to start fresh, and the search box to find an old conversation.
