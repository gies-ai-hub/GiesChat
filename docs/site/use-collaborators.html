Collaborators, drafts & co-admins

Build an agent with your TAs and co-instructors without anyone overwriting what students currently get.

The three roles

RoleCanCan't
Owner (you)Everything: edit, post drafts, embed, see usage, delete, change roles.—
Co-adminEdit the agent, post drafts, manage its embed link, see how the class uses it.Delete the agent or change roles.
CollaboratorMake their own draft copy, edit it, share a test link.Change what students get, unless the owner or a co-admin posts their draft.

How drafts work

  1. A collaborator chooses Start a draft. This makes their own copy of the agent, including its documents. Students never see it.
  2. They edit the instructions, model or documents, and can share a Test link that lets anyone try the draft without logging in.
  3. The owner or a co-admin opens the Drafts panel and chooses Post to production.
  4. The draft replaces what students get (instructions, model and documents) as a new version. The old version stays in version history.
Tip: if production changed after a draft was started, the draft is marked "production has changed since," so you don't silently undo someone else's edit.
