Using GiesChat › Tools

Tools

Tools let GiesChat reach outside the chat: read your Canvas courses, check your Outlook, build slide decks or apps. Turn them on from the tools button in the message box.

Canvas

Ask about your courses, assignments and due dates. The first time you use it, GiesChat asks for your own Canvas access token (Canvas → Account → Settings → New access token). The token is stored for your account only.

Outlook students only

Connect your Illinois Outlook to ask about mail, calendar and contacts, e.g. "What's on my calendar Thursday?" Access is read-only, so GiesChat can't send or delete anything. It is currently limited to student accounts.

Deck Builder

Builds a PowerPoint deck from a conversation:

  1. Answer a short question card: audience, length and purpose.
  2. Choose the Gies template or upload your own design.
  3. Review the slide preview, ask for changes, then download the .pptx.

App Builder

The App Builder agent asks a few brainstorming questions about the app you want, then has Replit build it, with a live preview right in the chat. It needs a free personal Replit account, which you connect once.

Course Tutors & Career Prep

In Agents, course tutors cover Gies courses, grounded in each course's own pages. Career Prep asks for your resume in a short intake form, then helps with interviews and applications.
