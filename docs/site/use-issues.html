Reporting an issue

Hit a bug or something feeling off? The bug button at the top of every chat sends a report straight to the GiesChat team.

Send a report

Report an issue
What happened?
"I uploaded my FIN 300 notes to the agent and it says it can't find anything about duration."
Analyze and submit

Describe what you were trying to do, what you expected and what went wrong. Then choose Analyze and submit.

What happens next

  1. GiesChat checks recent server warnings and errors linked to your account and shows an initial diagnosis. Raw logs are never shown to you or included in the report.
  2. You get a reference number. Mention it if you follow up.
  3. The report is filed with the development team, who track and fix it.
